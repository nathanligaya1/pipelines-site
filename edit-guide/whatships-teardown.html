22 motion effects from What Ships

I went through all 60 motion videos on whatships.com (a library of startup launch films). I studied the 52 short films frame by frame and spot-checked the 8 long podcasts. These are the best 22 effects.

Each one has 3 parts: the original (a short cut from the real video), our version (live, tap Replay), and how to ask for it in one line. For the basics (pop-in, karaoke captions, lower thirds), see Motion graphics.

Clips are short excerpts for study. Every clip links back to its page on whatships.com and the original post on X. Demos use our own text.
