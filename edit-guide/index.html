How to ask for an edit

One request = where + what + how much.

[timestamp]+[shot or effect term]+[how long / how strong]
  • 0:12, slow push-in on Jerome for 3s, subtle.Where, what, how long, how strong. Perfect.
  • 0:09, punch in on "million", about 30% tighter.Name the word the effect lands on.
  • 0:17 to 0:20, full-screen takeover on Jerome, then back to split.Give a start and end time for anything that lasts.
  • Captions: 2 to 3 words at a time, key word red with glow, subtle pop-in.For a whole-clip style, skip the timestamp and say "all captions".
  • 0:40, dip to black before the last line, 0.5s.Short transitions: give the length in seconds.

Strength and speed words

Use these so "a little" means the same thing to everyone.

SayMeans
SubtleYou feel it more than see it. Zoom 5 to 10%, pop 95 to 102%.
MediumClearly visible. Zoom 15 to 25%.
Big / hardImpossible to miss. Zoom 30%+, big overshoot.
Slow2 to 4 seconds.
Quick0.3 to 0.5 seconds.
InstantOn the cut, zero frames of motion (a punch-in).

Tips

  • Say who: "on JMac" or "on Jerome".
  • Timestamps come from the draft you are watching, not the full episode.
  • Say what you don't want too: "no sound effects", "no boxes behind captions".
  • Not sure of the word? Send the timestamp plus "like the ___ demo in the edit guide".
  • One change per line. Numbered lists are easiest to work through.
